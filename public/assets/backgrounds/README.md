## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: MINIMAL unobtrusive background image for a bright cheerful travel board game, wide landscape 16:9. The entire central 90 percent must be a very simple smooth pastel sky-blue to pale aqua gradient, EMPTY, flat color, almost no detail. ONLY three tiny, softly rendered decorative accents at the extreme outer edges: a little flat rounded palm-tree island silhouette partly cropped in the far bottom left corner, two small pillowy white clouds in the far top right, and a very tiny simple paper airplane with one faint dashed curved trail in the far top left. Clean simple low-detail 3D/vector hybrid casual game style, matte rounded forms, very soft light, low contrast and pale colors. Negative space is the whole point: the interactive board will cover the center and must get all attention. Think serene, airy, lightweight game background rather than scenic illustration. NO water waves, NO ripples, NO realistic textures, NO coastal town, NO detailed buildings, NO boats, NO beach objects, NO hot air balloons, NO scenery in the middle, NO board, NO UI, NO text, NO watermark, NO vivid busy colors, NO dark colors.
